### Role Context
- Frame as experienced business professional
- Specify industry or domain if relevant
- Consider organizational level

### Structure
Define logical flow:
- Situation (context and background)
- Impact (why this matters)
- Action (what needs to happen)
- Outcome (expected results)

### Tone
- Specify professional, solution-focused communication
- Balance confidence with humility
- Appropriate formality level

### Stakeholder Focus
- Consider audience needs and concerns
- Address potential objections
- Align with business priorities

### Clarity
- Request specific details (dates, metrics, actions)
- Use concrete language
- Avoid jargon unless necessary
- Quantify when possible

### Accountability
- Balance transparency with confidence
- Own challenges while showing solutions
- Clear ownership and responsibilities

### Next Steps
- Include clear actions
- Specify timelines
- Define success criteria
- Follow-up plan
