# SKILL FILE: BUSINESS

## WHEN TO USE THIS SKILL

Use this skill when the request involves:
- Business strategy or planning
- Market analysis or competitive research
- Financial analysis or projections
- Business model design
- Go-to-market strategies
- Performance metrics and KPIs
- Business case development
- Investment analysis
- Organizational strategy
- Pricing strategy
- Sales strategies or processes

**Keywords that trigger this skill:**
strategy, business, market, revenue, ROI, analysis, plan, growth, sales, metrics, KPI, competitive, financial, profit, cost, customer, pricing, valuation, investment

---

## OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST

When optimizing business prompts, ensure you've addressed:

✓ **Business Context Defined?**
  - Industry and market specifics
  - Company size and stage (startup, growth, enterprise)
  - Competitive landscape
  - Current challenges or opportunities

✓ **Objectives Clear?**
  - What business decision needs to be made?
  - What metrics matter (growth, profitability, market share)?
  - What timeframe (short-term tactics vs long-term strategy)?

✓ **Stakeholders Identified?**
  - Who will use this analysis?
  - What's their knowledge level (C-suite, managers, board)?
  - What level of detail do they need?

✓ **Data and Constraints Specified?**
  - What data is available?
  - What are the resource constraints (budget, time, team)?
  - What assumptions can be made?

✓ **Deliverable Format Defined?**
  - Executive summary, detailed report, presentation deck?
  - What sections are required?
  - How should data be presented (tables, charts)?

✓ **Business Logic Applied?**
  - Financial considerations (costs, revenue, margins)
  - Market dynamics (competition, trends, regulations)
  - Risk factors
  - Implementation feasibility

## TECHNIQUE APPLICATION

**Primary Techniques for Business Tasks:**

1. **Role Assignment**
   - Be specific about business expertise
   - Example: "You are a management consultant specializing in SaaS go-to-market strategy for Series A startups"
   - Not just: "You are a business expert"

2. **Context Layering**
   - Industry context: market size, growth rate, trends
   - Competitive context: key players, positioning, market dynamics
   - Company context: stage, resources, capabilities, constraints
   - Strategic context: goals, timeline, success metrics

3. **Chain-of-Thought**
   - Request structured analysis: "First analyze the market, then competitive positioning, then financial viability, finally provide recommendations"
   - Essential for complex business decisions
   - Ensures logical reasoning is transparent

4. **Success Criteria**
   - Define what "good" looks like in business terms
   - Example: "Recommendations should increase revenue by 20%+ while maintaining 70%+ gross margins"
   - Make outcomes measurable and time-bound

5. **Constraints**
   - Budget limitations
   - Time to market requirements
   - Resource availability
   - Regulatory requirements
   - Risk tolerance

## COMMON IMPROVEMENTS

### ❌ BAD: Vague Strategy Prompt
```
"Help me with a business strategy"
```

### ✅ GOOD: Optimized Strategy Prompt
```
You are a management consultant specializing in B2B SaaS growth strategy for early-stage startups.

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
- Industry: Project management software for remote teams
- Company: Series A startup, $3M ARR, 40 employees
- Market: Competitive (Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp), growing 25% YoY
- Current Position: Strong product-market fit with design agencies and marketing teams
- Challenge: How to scale from $3M to $10M ARR in 18 months

TASK:
Develop a go-to-market strategy to 3x ARR in 18 months while maintaining capital efficiency.

STAKEHOLDER:
- CEO and founding team
- Need: Clear roadmap for scaling revenue
- Constraints: $2M in runway, lean team, can't outspend competitors on marketing

REQUIREMENTS:
- Analysis Focus:
  - Current customer segments and their CAC/LTV
  - Market opportunities (segments, geographies, use cases)
  - Competitive positioning and differentiation
  - Channel effectiveness and scalability
- Timeframe: 18-month plan with quarterly milestones
- Output Format:
  - Executive summary (1 page)
  - Situation analysis (current state)
  - Strategic options (3-4 paths with pros/cons)
  - Recommended approach with detailed rationale
  - Implementation roadmap (quarterly)
  - Resource requirements and financial projections
- Key Metrics: ARR growth, CAC, LTV, gross retention, sales efficiency

ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK:
1. Analyze current state: customer segments, unit economics, growth drivers
2. Identify growth levers: new segments, expansion revenue, pricing, channels
3. Evaluate strategic options: ICP focus, product-led vs sales-led, geographic expansion
4. Model financial outcomes: revenue, costs, margins, runway
5. Recommend path with highest probability of success

DELIVERABLES:
- Clear recommendation: which segment(s) to target, which channels to prioritize
- Quarterly milestones: revenue targets, hiring plan, product launches
- Resource allocation: how to deploy the team and budget
- Risk assessment: what could derail the plan, mitigation strategies

SUCCESS CRITERIA:
- Strategy is achievable with current resources
- Financial model shows path to $10M ARR within 18 months
- Recommendations are specific and actionable (not generic advice)
- Risk factors are identified with mitigation plans
- Team can start executing immediately based on this plan
```

### ❌ BAD: Unclear Analysis Prompt
```
"Analyze this market opportunity"
```

### ✅ GOOD: Optimized Analysis Prompt
```
You are a market research analyst with expertise in B2C fintech and emerging payment technologies.

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
- Industry: Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) for e-commerce
- Company: Fintech startup considering entry into European market
- Market: Dominated by Klarna and Afterpay, but fragmented by country
- Challenge: Determining if and where to enter the European BNPL market

TASK:
Conduct a market opportunity analysis for BNPL services in Europe, focusing on 3-5 most attractive countries.

STAKEHOLDER:
- Executive team and potential investors
- Decision: Go/no-go on European expansion, and if go, which markets first
- Timeline: Need analysis in 2 weeks for investor pitch

REQUIREMENTS:
- Analysis Scope:
  - Market size and growth by country (TAM, SAM, SOM)
  - Competitive landscape by market (players, market share, positioning)
  - Regulatory environment (licensing, consumer protection laws)
  - Consumer adoption and preferences
  - Merchant adoption and economics
- Data Sources: Use publicly available data, analyst reports, regulatory filings
- Comparison: Benchmark against 3-4 established players (Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal Credit)
- Output Format:
  - Executive summary with clear recommendation
  - Country-by-country assessment (top 5)
  - Competitive analysis matrix
  - Entry strategy recommendations
  - Risk assessment

ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK:
1. Market sizing: Calculate TAM/SAM/SOM for each country
2. Competitive analysis: Map existing players, their strengths, market gaps
3. Regulatory assessment: Identify barriers to entry, compliance requirements
4. Consumer behavior: Adoption rates, preferences, demographics
5. Economic analysis: Unit economics, merchant fees, consumer costs
6. Strategic fit: Which markets align with company capabilities

DELIVERABLES:
- Market rankings: Top 3-5 countries with rationale
- Entry strategy: Organic build vs partnerships vs acquisition
- Investment requirements: Rough estimates for each market
- Timeline: Suggested pace of expansion
- Key risks and mitigation strategies

SUCCESS CRITERIA:
- Recommendation is data-driven (not just intuition)
- Includes specific market size estimates (€ amounts)
- Identifies concrete competitive advantages needed to win
- Financial projections are realistic (CAC, LTV, payback period)
- Investors can make informed decision based on this analysis
```

### ❌ BAD: Generic Pricing Prompt
```
"Help me figure out pricing for my product"
```

### ✅ GOOD: Optimized Pricing Prompt
```
You are a pricing strategist with expertise in SaaS subscription models and value-based pricing.

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
- Product: AI-powered email marketing tool for e-commerce brands
- Company: Startup, currently 50 paying customers at $99/month flat rate
- Market: Competing with Mailchimp ($20-350/mo), Klaviyo ($20-1700/mo), others
- Challenge: Current pricing is too simple, leaving money on the table with large customers

TASK:
Design a pricing strategy that maximizes revenue and aligns price with customer value.

CURRENT STATE:
- Product: Automated email sequences, AI personalization, analytics
- Customers: E-commerce brands, 90% on Shopify
- Sizes: 5 customers do $1M+/year GMV, 20 do $100K-1M, 25 do <$100K
- Current Price: $99/month flat, all features included
- Churn: 8% monthly, mostly small customers
- Feedback: Large customers say we're "a steal", small customers say "a bit pricey"

TASK:
Develop a new pricing model with 3-4 tiers that:
1. Captures more value from large customers
2. Remains affordable for small customers
3. Creates clear upgrade incentives
4. Aligns with customer value (revenue generated)

REQUIREMENTS:
- Pricing Model:
  - Define 3-4 pricing tiers
  - Specify price points for each tier
  - Define what features/limits per tier
  - Specify pricing metric (flat, per email, per revenue, hybrid)
- Analysis:
  - Customer segmentation by value
  - Revenue impact modeling (current vs proposed)
  - Competitive positioning per tier
  - Migration strategy (how to move existing customers)
- Constraints:
  - Can't price out current small customers
  - Must be simpler than Klaviyo's complex pricing
  - Should create 30%+ revenue uplift
  - Must be easy to explain in 30 seconds

ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK:
1. Segment customers by value metrics (GMV, emails sent, revenue generated for them)
2. Analyze willingness to pay per segment
3. Define value metrics (what drives value for customers?)
4. Design tier structure (features, limits, pricing)
5. Model revenue impact (expansion, churn, new customer acquisition)
6. Competitive comparison (where do we fit?)

DELIVERABLES:
- Pricing tier structure:
  - Tier names (Starter, Growth, Pro, Enterprise)
  - Price points ($ per tier)
  - Feature/limit differentiation
  - Target customer per tier
- Revenue modeling:
  - Current MRR: $4,950 (50 * $99)
  - Projected MRR: [calculate]
  - Assumptions: [upgrade %, churn impact, new acquisition]
- Migration plan:
  - How to transition existing customers
  - Grandfathering strategy
  - Communication approach
- Competitive positioning:
  - Where each tier sits vs competitors
  - Key differentiation

SUCCESS CRITERIA:
- Pricing aligns with customer value (large customers pay more)
- Creates at least 3 clear upgrade reasons between tiers
- Projected revenue increase of 30%+ within 6 months
- Churn risk is manageable (< 15% during transition)
- Pricing is simple enough for customers to self-select
- Sales team can explain and defend pricing easily
```

## DOMAIN-SPECIFIC TIPS

### For Strategy Work:
- Always define timeframe (1 year, 3 years, 5 years)
- Request specific frameworks (SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, Ansoff Matrix)
- Include competitive analysis
- Specify resource constraints

### For Financial Analysis:
- Request specific metrics (revenue, margins, CAC, LTV, payback period)
- Define assumptions clearly
- Ask for sensitivity analysis (what if scenarios)
- Specify confidence levels for projections

### For Market Analysis:
- Define market clearly (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- Request segmentation by relevant criteria
- Include competitive landscape
- Specify data sources and time period

### For Business Plans:
- Define audience (investors, board, internal team)
- Specify sections needed (exec summary, market, financials, etc.)
- Request realistic projections (not hockey sticks)
- Include risk factors and mitigation

### For Operational Strategy:
- Define current state and desired state
- Specify KPIs and targets
- Request implementation timeline
- Include resource requirements

## ANTI-PATTERNS TO AVOID

❌ Don't say: "Analyze the market"
✅ Instead: "Analyze the US project management software market: size, growth rate, key segments, top 5 competitors, market trends, and opportunities for differentiation"

❌ Don't say: "Create a business plan"
✅ Instead: "Create a 3-year business plan for a B2B SaaS startup targeting Series A: exec summary, market analysis, go-to-market strategy, financial projections (revenue, costs, margins), milestones, and use of funds"

❌ Don't say: "Good ROI"
✅ Instead: "Minimum 30% IRR over 3 years, with payback period under 18 months"

❌ Don't say: "Competitive advantage"
✅ Instead: "Sustainable competitive advantage based on proprietary technology, 2+ year lead time to replicate, protected by 3 patents, evidenced by 80%+ gross margins and <5% churn"

❌ Don't say: "Growth strategy"
✅ Instead: "Customer acquisition strategy to grow from $5M to $20M ARR in 24 months through: (1) outbound sales to enterprise, (2) product-led growth for SMB, (3) partnership channel with agencies, with CAC targets and ROI projections for each"

## REMEMBER

For business prompts:
- Context is everything (industry, company stage, competitive landscape)
- Quantify objectives (revenue targets, growth rates, metrics)
- Specify timeframes (short-term vs long-term)
- Define success in business terms (ROI, market share, profitability)
- Include constraints (budget, team, time, regulations)
- Request structured analysis with clear recommendations
- Always ask for the "so what" (actionable insights, not just data)

Business decisions require data, analysis, and clear recommendations. The more specific the context and objectives, the more actionable the output.
