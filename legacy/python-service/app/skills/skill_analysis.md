🚨 ANALYSIS PROMPT OPTIMIZATION 🚨

**ABSOLUTE RULE**: You are writing INSTRUCTIONS for performing analysis, NOT performing the analysis itself!

❌ NEVER OUTPUT ANALYSIS LIKE THIS:
"Here is the analysis of your sales data: Q1 revenue increased by 15%..."

✅ ALWAYS OUTPUT INSTRUCTIONS LIKE THIS:
"Act as a data analyst. Analyze the following sales data to identify key trends. Structure your report with: (1) Executive Summary, (2) Revenue Growth Analysis..."

## Optimize by Adding:

### Role Context
- Frame as senior analyst with domain expertise
- Specify industry or domain knowledge if relevant

### Chain of Thought
- Request step-by-step analytical approach
- Encourage showing work and reasoning

### Framework
Provide structure:
- Overview → Trends → Root Causes → Insights → Recommendations
- Executive summary at the beginning
- Detailed findings in body

### Depth Requirements
- Request specific numbers, percentages, comparisons
- Ask for quantitative evidence
- Require data-driven conclusions

### Output Structure
- Organize findings logically
- Use clear sections and headers
- Include executive summary
- Prioritize actionable insights

### Actionable Insights
- Emphasize practical recommendations
- Link insights to business impact
- Suggest next steps

### Visualization
- Suggest appropriate charts/graphs if relevant
- Specify data presentation format

## Example Transformation

**Example**: "analyze sales data"
**Optimized**: "You are a senior business analyst. Analyze the sales data to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities. Structure your analysis as follows: (1) Executive Summary with key findings, (2) Overall sales trends over time with percentage changes, (3) Top performing products/categories with specific metrics, (4) Underperforming areas with root cause analysis, (5) Customer segment analysis, (6) Actionable recommendations with expected impact. Include specific numbers, percentages, and comparisons. Suggest appropriate visualizations (line charts for trends, bar charts for comparisons). Present findings in a clear, scannable format with headers."
